\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

This section presents and discusses the results obtained from the analysis of 34 primary studies on LLM-based steganography. The findings are organized according to the five research questions defined in Section~\ref{sec:design}. For each question, we summarize the observed trends and interpret what they indicate about the current state of the field.

The overall taxonomy of the reviewed LLM-based approaches is summarized in Figure~\ref{fig:sunburst_chart}, which provides a compact visual anchor for the method families discussed across the subsections below.

\begin{figure*}[h!]
	\centering
	\includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]{sunburst_chart.pdf}
	\caption{Sunburst chart of LLM approaches.}
	\Description{A sunburst chart displaying the hierarchical breakdown of large language model approaches used in steganography research. Each segment represents a category or subcategory of LLM-based steganographic techniques identified in the literature review.}
	\label{fig:sunburst_chart}
\end{figure*}
